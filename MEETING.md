## What a good brief does

- Says in one line what you want out of the call.
- Explains each attendee's role and how long they have held it, so you pitch at the right level.
- Names the buyers who are not on the invite, so you can ask who else decides.
- Gives 2 to 4 talking points and 2 to 4 questions, each tied to one fact about the account or a person.
